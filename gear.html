Gears

Gears are the tools and equipment that players can use to enhance their abilities and survive in the game.

Utility gears are the gears that provide utility and support to the player.

Thrusts yourself in air, activates in midair

Slows your fall speed

Climb on the enemies or pull them to you, also can steal bobbys shield

Refreshers are the gears that provide temporary boosts or healing to the player.

Restores 50% of your health

Recharges every gear you have

If you drink it, from every hit you do u heal yourself.

Decreases gear and weapon cooldowns but impairs your vision and ability to aim

Active gears are the gears that provide active abilities to the player.

Creates a clone that can explode at death.

Reflects incoming projectiles.

Adds 125 hp to yourself but movement and jump speed are reduced.

Provides a temporary boost to your attack damage, but sets you on fire for 5 seconds.

Passive gears are the gears that provide passive abilities to the player.

Adds 50 hp to yourself.

Increases your health regeneration speed.

Spawns zombie from every ten kills.

Can or reduce or increase your damage by random percents.

Saves you from death and adds extra health.
